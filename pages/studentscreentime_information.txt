====== Student Screen Time Information ======

Here is what we have been told by teachers in the decades Rhino has been taught in classrooms all around the world.

Our foundational "version agnostic" training materials built into Rhino [[https://wiki.mcneel.com/rhino/trainingguides/en|here]].
They are available in PDF, HTML. And there are test that parallel the training material here.
However, you can find others and write your own, if you prefer.

To network with teachers, this is the [[https://rhino3du.ning.com/|best website]] to access the [[https://wiki.mcneel.com/people/rhino5cir|curriculum guide]], post questions and see example of [[https://rhino3du.ning.com/page/tutorials-1|class projects]] from teachers.
These [[https://rhino3d.education/|courses]] can be used in a self paced learning environment. You can ask [[jackie@mcneel.com|Jackie Nasser]] from our Miami office who oversees the Learning site.

Here are our answers to the best of our knowledge. Make sure and ask other teachers for their input on the ning web site.

==== Elementary Level ====

  * **Recommended usage time per instructional session**: 30 min.
  * **Recommended number of sessions per week:** 1-2 sessions/week
  * **Typical weekly usage based on your implementation recommendations:** Rhino is excellent to teach geometric shapes. Just making solids primitives, rotating the screen, assigning color, and screen capture or print a picture.
  * **Summary:** At this grade level, Rhino is intended for **occasional use**.

==== Middle School Level ====
  * **Recommended usage time per instructional session** 45 min.
  * **Recommended number of sessions per week:** 2-3 sessions/week
  * **Typical weekly usage based on your implementation recommendations:** Make small project to model in Rhino and 3D print. Coasters, key chains with school logo, cell phone holder. Fun simpler model with a design purpose. The user of 3D printer or laser cutter will be "eye opening".
  * **Summary:** At this grade level, Rhino is intended for supplemental use.

==== High School Level ====
  * **Recommended usage time per instructional session:** 1-2 hrs
  * **Recommended number of sessions per week:** 3-4/ week
  * **Typical weekly usage based on your implementation recommendations:** Do the [[https://wiki.mcneel.com/rhino/trainingguides/en|Level 1 & 2 Training guide.]]

    * Supplement with the [[https://wiki.mcneel.com/gettingstartedvideos|Getting Started projects]] (fast paced, 2hrs blank screen to model.)and other projects that you find.
    * Test with the [[https://wiki.mcneel.com/rhino/level1_test|Level 1 & 2 Certification Testing]].
  * Some advanced students may get the Level 2.
    * Others will want to investigate [[https://www.rhino3d.com/learn/?query=kind:%20grasshopper&modal=null|Grasshopper for parametric design]], especially if they are in advanced math/programming classes.
  * Always combine 3D printing, CNC or laser cutting with Rhino projects if the machines are available to the students.
  * At this grade level, Rhino is intended for daily use.
  * We believe it is a realistic guide line, however, there are many variation of this that will also work for certain groups of students.
